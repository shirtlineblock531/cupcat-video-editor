## 📖 Your First Video: A Quick Guide

### Step 1: Open the App

Double-click the app icon. The main screen will appear with a clean, simple interface.

### Step 2: Create a New Project

Click the **"New Project"** button. Give your project a name and choose where to save it.

### Step 3: Import Your Video

Click **"Import Media"** and select one or more video files from your computer. They'll appear in the media library.

### Step 4: Add to Timeline

Drag your video clips from the media library down to the timeline at the bottom of the screen.

### Step 5: Use AI Subtitles

Click the **"Generate Subtitles"** button. The AI will listen to your video and create subtitles automatically. You can edit the text, change fonts, and adjust timing if needed.

### Step 6: Ask Claude for Help

Want to cut a section? Reorder clips? Add a transition? Just type a command like:

- *"Remove the first 5 seconds"*
- *"Move clip 3 to the beginning"*
- *"Add a fade-in at the start"*

Claude will make the changes directly on your timeline.

### Step 7: Export Your Video

When you're happy with your edit, click **"Export"**. Choose your desired quality and format, then wait for the render to finish. Your video is ready to share!
